Sponsorship Invitation: Headcorn International Airshow, 25–27 June 2027

I am writing to invite Brands Hatch Morgans to partner with the Headcorn International Airshow in 2027. The show returns to Headcorn Aerodrome in Kent on Saturday 26 and Sunday 27 June, with our Futures & Arrivals Day on Friday 25 June.

Vintage cars on the flightline are part of what makes Headcorn different, and few cars sit as naturally beside a vintage warbird as a Morgan. Our visitors are enthusiasts with a love of British engineering and heritage, many of them from across Kent and the South East, which makes them the customers a Kent Morgan dealer wants to meet.

Headcorn is a heritage show on a working grass aerodrome. In 2026 we welcomed more than 10,000 visitors across the weekend, and more than 30 aircraft from 18 operators in four countries. Warbirds fly alongside vintage cars on the flightline, and our hospitality hosts guests including international air attachés.
