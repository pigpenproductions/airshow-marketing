# HIA 2027 sponsor outreach: new niche prospects

This work adds to the **HIA 2027 Sponsorship Pack** ([doc](https://claude.ai/artifact/WXv8yqpWNe6wjGdoK78TZb)). It uses the pack's packages, prices and email format. Nothing has been sent and nobody has been contacted.

## What's here
- **Pack doc, Prospects tab:** rows 51-65 added on 26 Nov. These are niche aviation, heritage-brand and on-airfield targets that weren't in the original 50.
- `outreach-emails.md`: tailored first emails for 51-58, each under 120 words, plus which standard pack email to use for 59-65.
- `sponsor-research.csv`: the evidence behind each new prospect, with sources and what to check before sending. Balfour, BAE, Cripps and Biddenden were already in the pack (rows 9, 3, 24 and 43).

## Best new picks
1. **Bremont:** Mustang Lounge partner (£7,500). They sponsored Flying Legends and back the Bremont Horsemen P-51 team. It's the strongest aviation-heritage brand fit in the UK.
2. **Gallagher Aerospace:** Fighter Row partner (£5,000). They insure air shows and vintage aircraft, so our operators are their clients.
3. **Footman James:** Display partner (£2,500), plus a stand by the vintage cars. Classic owners are their customers.
4. **Headcorn flying school:** Community supporter (£750) plus a trial-lesson prize. It's on the airfield and an easy yes.
5. **TotalEnergies:** fuel in kind plus a Display partnership. The pack already lists fuel as an in-kind category.
6. **Chilston Park or Mercure Great Danes:** crew and VIP rooms in kind. Pick one alongside Hotel du Vin (row 46).
7. **Brands Hatch Morgans:** Display or Community, with cars and test drives on site.
8. **Private White V.C.:** official outfitter for the flightline crew, in kind.
