# HIA 2027 sponsor outreach

Status: drafts for Alex. Nothing has been sent and nobody has been contacted.

## What's here
- `sponsor-targets.csv`: 20 targets, ranked. Each row has why they fit, the decision-maker's role, the angle, the tier, the likelihood, what to check before sending, and sources.
- `../../letters/sponsors/sponsor-proposal-2027.pdf`: generic proposal letter in the RJF format, with a tier panel in place of the event-details panel.
- `../../letters/sponsors/<target>.pdf`: 10 tailored letters. Each one marks a "Proposed" tier.
- `letters-data.js`: all letter text. Edit it, then run `node scripts/sponsor-letters.js` to rebuild the HTML and PDFs. The script checks each letter still fits on one page.

## Top picks
1. **Shepherd Neame**: Spitfire ale, the Spitfire Ground, and about £500k a year in sponsorship. Already an HIA partner, so this is an upgrade conversation. Pitch Presenting Partner.
2. **Balfour Winery (Hush Heath, Staplehurst)**: a few miles away. Pitch Hospitality: Balfour poured in premium hospitality and the Mustang Lounge.
3. **BAE Systems Rochester**: Kent's largest private employer, with STEM Ambassadors. Pitch the Futures & Arrivals Day.
4. **Bremont**: the UK's best aviation-heritage brand fit (Flying Legends, the Fighter Collection, the Bremont Horsemen). Pitch a named warbird display.
5. **Footman James**: classic car insurer. Pitch a named vintage car flightline.
6. **Gallagher Aerospace**: insures air shows and vintage aircraft. Pitch an owners' and operators' enclosure.
7. **Private White V.C.**: heritage flight jackets. Pitch official outfitter for the flightline crew (in kind plus fee).
8. **Cripps**: a Kent law firm that already sponsors the Wine Garden of England. Pitch client hospitality.
9. **Chilston Park Hotel**: about 5 miles away. Pitch Official Hotel (rooms in kind plus fee).
10. **TotalEnergies Aviation**: UL91 avgas, and backs aerobatic pilots. Pitch a named aerobatic display. This is a long shot.

The fastest money is probably the local ones: Shepherd Neame, Balfour and Chilston Park. Bremont and BAE are the biggest prizes, and they need the longest lead time, so send those first.
